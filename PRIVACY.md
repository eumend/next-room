# Next Room privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Next Room is a free offline game by RockCandy.

- The game does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.
- It requests no permissions on Android or iOS and does not use the internet.
- Your save file (your progress in the game) stays on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/eumend/next-room/issues or leave a comment at https://rockcandy.itch.io/nextroom.
