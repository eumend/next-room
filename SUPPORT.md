# Next Room support

Next Room is a free offline game by RockCandy.

## Get help
- Email: hello@eumend.com
- Bug reports and ideas: https://github.com/eumend/next-room/issues

## Common questions
- **Where is my progress saved?** On your device only. It saves after every room; tap CONTINUE on the title screen to resume.
- **How do I start over?** Tap START on the title screen. This replaces your saved run.
- **Does the game collect data?** No. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
